Welcome to Taniti

Explore the Tropical Island of Taniti

Taniti is a small, tropical island in the Pacific. While the island has an area of less than 500 square miles, the terrain is varied and includes both white and yellow sand beaches, a large, predominantly-flat harbor, a small active volcano, and an interior of lush, tropical rainforest.

Frequently Requested Info

Currency

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar as its currency. Many businesses also accept euros and yen, and many businesses accept major credit cards.

  • Taniti uses the U.S. dollar as its currency.
  • Many businesses also accept euros and yen.
  • Many businesses accept major credit cards.
🔌
Electrical Standards

Electrical Outlets

Taniti uses 120-volt electrical outlets (the same as in the United States).

  • 120-volt electrical outlets are standard across the island.
  • Devices standard in the United States plug directly into Taniti outlets.
🗣️
Communication

Language

Many Tanitians speak English, which is taught in schools, although Tanitian is also spoken.

  • English is taught in schools and spoken by many Tanitians.
  • Tanitian is also spoken on the island.
🏥
Medical Services

Medical Facilities

Taniti has one hospital and several clinics. The hospital has English-speaking staff.

  • One hospital is located on the island.
  • The hospital has English-speaking staff.
  • Several clinics provide additional medical care.

Safety Information

Safety and Crime

Violent crime is rare on Taniti, but petty crime like pickpocketing does occur.

  • Violent crime is rare on Taniti.
  • Petty crime like pickpocketing does occur; visitors should take standard precautions.

Holiday Information

National Holidays

Many businesses close on national holidays, so visitors should plan accordingly.

  • Business closures occur during national holidays.

Regulations

Drinking Age and Alcohol Regulations

The legal drinking age on Taniti is 21. Alcohol is not served or sold between the hours of midnight and 9:00 a.m.

  • Legal drinking age is 21.
